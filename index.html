03 / Make the case

A useful estimate shows its work.

Cost alone cannot tell you whether a service is better. The full planning journey will connect the chosen route to workload volume, operating cost, staff capacity, service measures, and the evidence a pilot must collect.

  1. 01
    Define the baseline

    How is the work done today, and what does success mean for residents and staff?

  2. 02
    Model cost transparently

    Use current, dated inputs and the right cost method for each Claude route.

  3. 03
    Test the value

    Measure quality, time returned, adoption, and service outcomes before scaling.
