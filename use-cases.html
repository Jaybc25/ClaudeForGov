How this catalog was made

The featured set is an editorial shortlist of bounded, measurable pilot ideas, not a ranking based on demonstrated outcomes. All examples are illustrative. Some problem areas were informed by the separate JayAI inventory, but NVIDIA-specific blueprint content was not carried over. Organization and department tags indicate possible fit, and Claude route suggestions remain hypotheses to test in discovery.
