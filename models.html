What the token estimate leaves out

Platform and delivery

Integration, hosting, retrieval, logging, evaluations, security review, support, and staff time.

Pricing variations

Prompt caching, Batch API, tools, long-context or fast modes, data residency, partner platforms, taxes, and negotiated terms.

Real workload behavior

Retries, tool calls, changing prompt length, output variation, and model-specific tokenization. Measure actual usage and review invoices.
